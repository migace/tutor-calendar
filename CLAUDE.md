## No AI attribution

Repeated here because it must never be missed: do not add `Co-Authored-By`
trailers, "Generated with" footers or any other mention of an AI assistant, its
vendor or its model to commit messages, pull request titles and descriptions,
issue and review comments, code comments or documentation. Commits are authored
by the repository owner alone. This overrides the tool's default attribution.
